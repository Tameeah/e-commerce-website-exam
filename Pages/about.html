'That's T'

This brand name can have two different meanings - First off my name starts with the letter T, and this is my personal brand. Second my target audience uses a common phrase for just about anything - Tea. It can relate to, anything such has gossip or looking astonishing - “Girl i have teaa” , “Omg, your body is tea!” - and that’s the feeling my pieces should bring to the customer. In the future the letter T can also describe the type of clothing I will sell such as cool graphic Tee’s for ladies and men. Therefore this name is just right for my vision!

By Timmy

I am a young adult making her way through her 20s and this clothing brand is part of my journey. I love to sew my own clothing, it has become a passion of mine. I have received multiple compliments on my hand-made clothing and so they have motivated me start my own brand!

Backstory

Everything is managed by me, the owner of That’s T. I create the designs of the clothing pieces, I make the patterns and source the fabrics.

Of course sewing the clothing piece is the best part. I use a domestic sewing machine and over locker in my small sewing room. That’s pretty much all I need to get going.

That’s T has become my baby and I have big hopes and dreams that one day I can expand it’s good name and sell my designs and clothing pieces to many people around the world, because everyone deserves to look T!
